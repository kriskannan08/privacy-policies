Privacy Policy

Your financial information is personal. This policy explains how GoDue handles information when you use the app to organise payments, manage reminders, and view expenses.

Our approach: GoDue is designed to manage your payment records on your device rather than through a GoDue-operated account or payment database.

Third-party services, such as advertising and currency conversion providers, may process certain technical information when you use their features. The details are explained below.

01 Information You Enter

GoDue allows you to record and organise information related to payments, bills, and recurring expenses. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Payment, bill, or service names
  • Payment categories
  • Payment amounts and currency codes
  • Due dates and recurrence schedules
  • Reminder preferences
  • Optional notes you choose to add
  • Payment status and payment dates

This information is used to provide the app's payment management, expense summary, and reminder features.

Please avoid entering passwords, bank account credentials, card numbers, or other highly sensitive information into payment names or notes.

02 Storage of Your Data

GoDue is designed to store payment records locally on your device using Apple's data storage technologies. GoDue does not operate its own user-account system or backend database for storing your payment records.

Your payment records and notes are not intentionally transmitted to GoDue-operated servers.

However, operating-system features such as device backups may store or process app data depending on your device settings and the applicable service provider's policies.

Information processed by third-party services is handled separately, as described in the relevant sections of this policy.

03 Notifications

GoDue can schedule local notifications to remind you about upcoming payments.

Notification timing is based on the due dates and reminder preferences configured in the app. Local notifications are scheduled through Apple's notification system.

You can allow, disable, or change notification permissions through your device settings.

04 Advertising and Google AdMob

GoDue uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK and related services may collect or process information to provide advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising operations.

Depending on the configuration, your location, applicable permissions, and your privacy choices, information processed by these services may include:

  • IP address and general network information
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where available
  • Device, operating-system, and app information
  • Advertising interactions and diagnostics
  • Information used to measure ad performance and prevent fraud

The exact information collected depends on the SDK configuration, the advertising services used, and applicable privacy requirements.

GoDue does not intentionally send the payment names, bill amounts, personal notes, or payment records you enter into the app to Google AdMob for advertising.

Google and its partners may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable requirements. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

05 Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

GoDue uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent where applicable.

Depending on your location and the legal requirements that apply, you may be presented with a consent or privacy message before advertising services are used.

Where required, you can make choices about the processing of information for advertising purposes through the consent interface provided by the app. Your choices may affect the types of advertisements that can be shown.

GoDue currently uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent where applicable. This consent flow is separate from Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. GoDue does not currently request tracking permission through Apple's ATT prompt. Advertising behaviour and the information processed by advertising services may vary according to your location, consent choices, SDK configuration, and applicable requirements.

If advertising privacy options are available in the app, you can use them to review or change your choices. The options shown depend on the consent configuration and requirements that apply to your location.

06 Currency Conversion

GoDue may use the Frankfurter service to retrieve exchange-rate information for currency conversion features.

When a currency-rate request is made, the service provider may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of normal network communication.

Payment records and personal notes are not intended to be sent to the currency-rate service.

For information about the service, visit Frankfurter.

07 Data Sharing

GoDue does not sell your payment records or personal notes.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to support features such as advertising and currency-rate retrieval.

Those providers may handle information according to their own privacy policies, service configurations, and applicable legal requirements.

08 Data Retention and Deletion

Payment records stored locally in GoDue generally remain on your device until you delete them through the app or remove the app and its associated data.

Actual deletion and retention may be affected by operating-system behaviour, device backups, or other device settings.

Information processed independently by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention policies and applicable requirements.

09 Children's Privacy

GoDue is intended as a payment and expense management tool and is not specifically designed for children.

The app's intended audience, advertising setup, consent messages, and data-processing practices should be appropriate for the regions in which the app is distributed.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is inconsistent with applicable requirements, please contact us so we can review the concern.

10 Security

GoDue is designed to keep payment records on your device rather than maintain a central GoDue payment database.

You should protect your device using an appropriate passcode, biometric authentication, and other available security features.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information processed by third-party providers. These may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You can manage locally stored payment records through the app's available features and manage device permissions through your device settings.

Where advertising consent or privacy controls are available, you can use those controls to review or change your choices.

Requests concerning information processed directly by a third-party provider may need to be directed to that provider.

12 Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when GoDue's features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when appropriate.
