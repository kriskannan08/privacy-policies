Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a payment?

Open GoDue and use the Add Payment option to enter your payment details, amount, due date, and other available preferences.

Can I manage recurring payments?

Yes. You can configure recurring schedules for regular bills and payments using the available recurrence options when setting up a payment.

How do I change my default currency?

Open Settings and select Default Currency to choose your preferred currency for new payments.

Where can I find previous payments?

Open Payment History to review your recorded payment activity.

Where can I manage privacy preferences?

If Privacy Options are available in your version of GoDue, you can access them from Settings.

Where are my payment records stored?

GoDue is designed to store your payment records locally on your device. For more information about data handling and third-party services, please read our Privacy Policy .
